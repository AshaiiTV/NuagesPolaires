# Critique visuelle — entree-agenda-registre-1

Note : 7/10

The group is close to the home page's standard: carnet pages (/entrer, /compte, /plus, /agenda) are clean, well-spaced and faithful to §5.6, with exact micro-texts such as « Ton compte attend sa liaison pour venir. », « Complet », « Tu viens · Rayer ma place », and night, Papier and 390 px all hold without overflow or console errors (only the expected 404 on /nexiste-pas). The Registre falls behind: in the audit log, unmapped Table actions print raw technical codes (« table_sauvee — id : c_… · detail : auto »), the banned word « staff » appears (« Journal du staff »), and the theme previews are clipped at 390 px. Next come several fidelity gaps: theme names and descriptions contradict §7, the organiser's stamp shows only a pseudo, a hidden rendez-vous is shown to the designer, and there are typographic slips such as « fiche de Aria », « 1 mai » and « COLLECTION· ».
