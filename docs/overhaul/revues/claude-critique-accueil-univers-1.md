# Critique visuelle — accueil-univers-1

Note : 6/10

The univers pages (synopsis, système, règlement, site et données, Serments) are solid. They have a clean double page, a sticky sommaire, readable phone tables, no overflow, no text under 12 px and no console error, and « tu es ici » shows on Aria's palier (Duelliste, branch A, niv. 7). Premiers pas, however, is a raw dump of the old page. It shows all five account states at once, unfilled placeholders (<pseudo>, <Nom du personnage>), action labels that are not links, and banned words (tableau de bord, l'équipe, événements), and it ignores the account state the contract requires. The home page, the quality reference, loses ground on phone (no visitor bottom band) and for a signed-in account (it still shows « Entrer » and « Rejoindre l'aventure »). Its sheets have no publisher stamp and send the visitor to a login. A circumflex placement bug in Cormorant (Mêlée, Rôdeur) is visible on / and on /univers/serments, and brass is used for chapter numbers.
